Wins so far, out of 1,000

How we found it: the robot tried 6,144 different rules. Each one had to pass three tests in a row, on three different stretches of gold history, without seeing the next one first. Only 9 passed, and the best ones were all the same idea: at night (Asia hours) when gold is calm, follow a breakout of the last 50 candles on the 15-minute chart, with a small target and a far safety stop.

In the replay from 2020 to 2026 it won 394 times and lost 26 times (94% wins), and it made money in every year tested, even with double fees. But each win is small (about $2) and each loss is about $20, so the total was only +$282 on $5,000 when risking 0.5% a trade (about $1,240 at the 2% it uses now). It takes about 150 trades a year, so reaching 1,000 wins live will take years. That is the real final exam.
